I use AI as a productivity tool, and I remain responsible for the final content. All AI-generated content is reviewed and edited for accuracy and compliance with IBM style guidelines.

## Content Cortex rebranding

<dl class="case-facts">
  <dt>Challenge</dt>
  <dd>Rename IBM FileNet Content Manager to IBM Content Cortex across the Cloud Pak for Business Automation documentation.</dd>
  <dt>My role</dt>
  <dd>Led the initiative.</dd>
  <dt>Approach</dt>
  <dd>Used conditional search and replace with AI to find and update the terminology.</dd>
  <dt>Quality assurance</dt>
  <dd>Reviewed and edited the changes for accuracy and compliance with IBM style guidelines.</dd>
</dl>

[See the rename in the 26.0.0 release notes](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/cloud-paks/cp-biz-automation/26.0.0?topic=notes-whats-new-in-2600#whatsnew__rename)
